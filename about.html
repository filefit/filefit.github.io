About FileFit

FileFit helps people prepare documents for online forms: photos, signatures and PDFs at exactly the size a form asks for.

Why it exists

Online applications for exams, jobs, admissions and government services in India usually ask for files within tight limits — a photo of 20–50 KB, a signature of 10–20 KB, PDFs under 100 or 200 KB. Getting this right with general-purpose tools takes trial and error, and many online compressors upload sensitive documents like ID proofs and marksheets to their servers. FileFit was built to make this one job quick and private.

How it works

Everything runs inside your web browser using open-source libraries (pdf.js and pdf-lib) and the browser's own image engine. FileFit searches for the highest image quality that still fits your size window, handles both 1000- and 1024-byte kilobytes, and never sends your files anywhere.

Who makes it

FileFit is built and maintained by Arnab Ray, a researcher in computer science based in Kolkata, India. Questions, bug reports and suggestions are welcome via the contact page.

Independence

FileFit is an independent tool and is not affiliated with SSC, UPSC, IBPS, any exam body or any government department. Sizes on this page are common examples: always follow the official notice of your form.

How FileFit is funded

The core tools are free. Optional FileFit Pro (batch processing, signing and filling PDFs) and ads on our guide pages help pay for development. The tool itself never shows ads.
