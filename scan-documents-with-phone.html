How to scan documents with your phone so they are clear and small

You don't need a scanner. A phone camera plus a few habits gives scans that are readable and small enough for any upload limit.

Take a good photo

  • Flat and straight: place the document on a dark table so the edges stand out; hold the phone directly above it.
  • Even light: daylight from the side, no flash (flash causes a bright glare spot), no shadow of your hand or phone.
  • Fill the frame: the document should cover most of the picture — empty table around it is wasted size.
  • Focus: tap on the text before taking the picture and hold still.

Many phones have a built-in document scanner (for example in the camera, notes or file app) that straightens pages automatically — use it if you have one.

Turn the photos into one PDF

Open Convert → Images to PDF in FileFit, add the photos in page order and choose A4 with margins. You get one PDF with one page per photo.

Make it fit the limit

Use Compress to size with the limit from your form. For text documents tick Black & white — it removes colour noise, keeps text sharp and often halves the size.
