Help for the file sizes and formats online forms ask for.

Exam forms (checked against official notices)

JEE Main 2027: photo, signature and document sizes

The JEE (Main) 2027 application asks for a scanned photo of 10–200 KB, a signature of 10–100 KB and a Class 10 certificate PDF of 50–300 KB, plus a live photo taken during the form.

NEET (UG): photo, signature, thumb impression and document sizes

The NEET (UG) 2026 bulletin asks for a photo of 10–200 KB, a signature of 10–100 KB, finger and thumb impressions of 10–200 KB and certificate PDFs of 50–300 KB.

CUET (UG): photo and signature sizes

The CUET (UG) 2026 bulletin asks for a scanned photo of 10–200 KB and a signature of only 10–50 KB — smaller than JEE and NEET, so a signature made for those may be rejected here.

IBPS PO: photo, signature, left thumb and handwritten declaration sizes

IBPS asks for four images, each with its own size: photo 200×230 px (20–50 KB), signature 140×60 px (10–20 KB), left thumb 240×240 px (20–50 KB) and handwritten declaration 800×400 px (50–100 KB).

UPSC online application: photo and signature sizes

UPSC's Common Application Form takes a JPG photo of 20–200 KB and a JPG signature of 20–100 KB. The signature is written three times, one below the other.
