Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Your files never leave your computer. Every PDF and image you add to FileFit is processed inside your browser. Files are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared. FileFit has no analytics or tracking.

Pro licence

Only if you activate Pro, FileFit contacts our licence server (hosted on Amazon Web Services) and sends your licence key, or the purchase email and payment ID you enter, and a random ID for your browser. For each licence we store the purchase email, plan, expiry date and up to three browser IDs. Our payment partners send us the purchase email, payment ID and amount when you buy.

Payments

Card, UPI and bank details are handled only by Razorpay or our USD payment partner under their privacy policies. We never receive them.

Your rights

Email us to see or delete your licence data. Deleting it ends your Pro access.

Extension permissions

  • contextMenus: adds "with FileFit" to the right-click menu.
  • storage: remembers your Pro licence in your browser.
  • Optional site access: asked only when you right-click a file on a website, so FileFit can open that file.
