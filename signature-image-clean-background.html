How to make a clean signature image for online forms

A signature upload looks simple but is easy to get wrong: grey paper, shadows and faint ink all make the result look messy. These steps give a clean result in a couple of minutes.

1. Sign properly

  • Use plain white paper with no lines.
  • Use a black or dark blue pen with an even flow — gel or fine felt pens work best.
  • Sign at your normal size, roughly in the middle of the page.

2. Photograph it

  • Stand near a window in daylight; avoid overhead lamps that cast your phone's shadow on the paper.
  • Hold the phone flat above the paper, parallel to it, so the signature is not stretched.
  • Fill most of the frame with the signature, leaving a small margin.

3. Size it

Open FileFit's signature preset, choose your photo, and set the width, height and KB range your form asks for. Use Fit inside so no part of the signature is cut off, and tick Black & white — this turns the grey background white and the strokes dark.

4. Check before uploading

  • Is the whole signature visible, with no strokes cut at the edges?
  • Is the background plain and even?
  • Is the file size inside the allowed range? FileFit shows the exact size under the preview.
