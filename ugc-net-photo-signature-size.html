UGC-NET: photo, signature and certificate sizes

The UGC-NET December 2026 bulletin asks for a scanned photo of 10–200 KB, a signature of 10–50 KB and, if applicable, a disability certificate PDF of 50–300 KB. A live photo is also captured.

Each Make it button opens FileFit with that exact size already filled in. Your file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. FileFit keeps the result inside the limit whether the portal counts 1 KB as 1000 or 1024 bytes.

Good to know

  • You also capture a live photograph in the form: light background, 80% face with ears, no mask.
  • Use the official site ugcnet.nta.nic.in; older bulletins on other NTA addresses are out of date.

Source

Updated Information Bulletin, UGC-NET December 2026 (16 October 2026), National Testing Agency — official website: https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/. Always read the latest official notice before you apply; exam bodies change these rules between years.
