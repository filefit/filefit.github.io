NEET (UG): photo, signature, thumb impression and document sizes

The NEET (UG) 2026 bulletin asks for a photo of 10–200 KB, a signature of 10–100 KB, finger and thumb impressions of 10–200 KB and certificate PDFs of 50–300 KB.

Each Make it button opens FileFit with that exact size already filled in. Your file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. FileFit keeps the result inside the limit whether the portal counts 1 KB as 1000 or 1024 bytes.

Good to know

  • A live photograph is also captured during the form.
  • These figures are from the 2026 bulletin. The 2027 bulletin was not out when this page was checked; compare with it when it is released.

Source

Information Bulletin, NEET (UG) 2026, National Testing Agency — official website: https://neet.nta.nic.in/. Always read the latest official notice before you apply; exam bodies change these rules between years.
