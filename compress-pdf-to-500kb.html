How to do it

  1. Click Open FileFit for this task (above).
  2. Choose or drop your file.
  3. Check the size settings (already filled in for this task) and press the button.
  4. Download the result. Your file never leaves your computer.

Tip: FileFit first tries a lossless squeeze, so text stays selectable whenever possible.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. All single-file tools are free. FileFit Pro adds batch processing and PDF signing.

Is my file uploaded?

No. FileFit runs entirely in your browser; your documents are never sent to any server.

What if the portal counts 1 KB as 1000 bytes?

FileFit stays under the limit for both meanings (1000 and 1024 bytes), so the file passes either check.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on phones and computers.
