UPSC online application: photo and signature sizes

UPSC's Common Application Form takes a JPG photo of 20–200 KB and a JPG signature of 20–100 KB. The signature is written three times, one below the other.

PhotographJPG20 KB – 200 KBnot fixedMake it
Signature (three times, one below the other)
Plain white paper, black ink pen.		JPG20 KB – 100 KBnot fixedMake it

Each Make it button opens FileFit with that exact size already filled in. Your file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. FileFit keeps the result inside the limit whether the portal counts 1 KB as 1000 or 1024 bytes.

Good to know

  • UPSC also asks you to capture a live photograph while filling the Common Application Form.
  • An irrelevant or wrong photo or signature can be treated as misconduct, so upload a clear, recent one.

Source

Examination Notice 08/2026 — CAPF (AC) Examination 2026, UPSC (the Civil Services 2026 notice 05/2026 uses the same Common Application Form) — official website: https://upsc.gov.in/. Always read the latest official notice before you apply; exam bodies change these rules between years.
