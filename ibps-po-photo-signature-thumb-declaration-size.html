IBPS PO: photo, signature, left thumb and handwritten declaration sizes

IBPS asks for four images, each with its own size: photo 200×230 px (20–50 KB), signature 140×60 px (10–20 KB), left thumb 240×240 px (20–50 KB) and handwritten declaration 800×400 px (50–100 KB).

Each Make it button opens FileFit with that exact size already filled in. Your file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. FileFit keeps the result inside the limit whether the portal counts 1 KB as 1000 or 1024 bytes.

Good to know

  • The form will not let you continue until the photo and signature match these specifications.
  • You also capture a live photograph by webcam or phone inside the form.
  • The declaration text to copy is printed in the notification. FileFit's “Fit inside” keeps the whole page of writing visible instead of cropping it.
  • IBPS Clerk, RRB and SO notifications use similar rules, but check each notice: sizes can differ.

Source

Detailed Notification, CRP PO/MT-XVI (30 June 2026), IBPS — official website: https://www.ibps.in/. Always read the latest official notice before you apply; exam bodies change these rules between years.
